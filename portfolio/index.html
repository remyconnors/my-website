Bringing ten regional stacks onto one platform

Ten regions each ran their own data stack, with their own mix of BI and pipeline tools. Every region paid for tools the others also paid for, reporting looked different everywhere, and because regions reported in different currencies, global numbers couldn't simply be summed.

Illustrative sequence. Region names and dates withheld.

  • Built the business case and a phased plan to move all ten regions onto one platform: Funnel.io for ingestion and Azure (Data Factory, Fabric) for storage and outputs.
  • Worked region by region and client by client: migrate historical data, map legacy campaign names and UTMs to our standards, train client teams on Funnel.io's self-service ingestion, and make outputs readily available to each team.
  • Connected a currency-conversion API to the pipeline, so figures from every region convert to a single currency and can be summed for global reporting.
  • Planned a protected window for legacy clients, so live reporting keeps running through the cutover.
  • Presented the strategy to leadership and wrote supporting materials for internal communication.

Cutting the number of BI and pipeline tools in use and standardizing across regions not only saves dozens of hours of manual work each reporting cycle and thousands of dollars in tool costs, but also enables us to create more useful and in-depth reporting for global leadership.
